Reading is mostly a matter of rhythm. A comfortable measure, generous leading, and a font that gets out of the way will do more for comprehension than any amount of visual ornament.

This post exists to verify that Latin text, mixed with `inline code` and math like $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, renders correctly at the same measure as CJK content.

## Measure and leading

The reading column caps out around 42em. For Latin text that works out to roughly 68 characters per line, which is the upper end of comfortable.

<Alert type="warning" title="Keep it readable">
  Line length is the single most common mistake in blog design. Too wide and the
  eye loses its place on the return sweep.
</Alert>
